Quick Tutorial

How To Read Your Orders

Use this board in under ten seconds: verify your slot, pick the active phase, tap your number on the map.

Step 1 / 4
Verify Your Slot

Open the Roster Check panel at the top and confirm which squad and team you belong to this week. Tap a team image to enlarge it and read player positions clearly.

Hit “Slot verified” once you've checked — the panel collapses for the rest of the session.
Step 1
Roster Check
Team 1 / Team 2 screenshots show exactly which slot you play. Always confirm before the match starts.
Pick The Active Phase

The phase tabs at the top of the map switch through Phase 1, Phase 2, Core and Phase 3. The map and every player position update automatically when you switch.

The tabs stay pinned to the top as you scroll — keep them handy mid-match.
Step 2
Phase Tabs
Match progression is chronological. Switch tabs as the timer advances rather than scrolling.
Tap Your Number On The Map

Every painted number on the map is a tappable hotspot. Tap your slot (A3, B5, D2…) and a panel slides up with your tasks for the currently active phase.

No more scrolling through a long task list — only your role's orders for the phase you're in.
Step 3
Map Hotspots
Numbers move between phases because positions move in the game. The map always shows the right setup.
Execute & Listen For Calls

Follow the tasks in order, watch the timestamps, and listen for the shot calls (Captain up, Core setup, ALL IN…). When a phase ends, switch the tab and tap your number again.

Tasks + map position + shot call timing = your full order.
Step 4
Execution
Close the task panel anytime by tapping the backdrop, swiping down, or pressing Escape.
